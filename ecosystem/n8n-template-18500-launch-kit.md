# How My ManyChat + n8n Automation Workflow Got Officially Published on n8n.io

As conversational marketing and AI direct messaging funnels become the backbone of modern lead acquisition, automation architects frequently hit a critical infrastructure limitation: **external webhook timeout limits**.

ManyChat—one of the most popular conversational frontends for Instagram, WhatsApp, and Facebook Messenger—enforces a strict 10-second timeout on HTTP request actions. When connecting ManyChat to AI reasoning models, CRM enrichment APIs, or multi-step database lookups, processing latency often exceeds 10 seconds, causing ManyChat to fail the node and freeze the conversation.

---

## The Core Technical Architecture

To ensure zero dropped conversations and 99.9% uptime, the workflow decouples the initial webhook handshake from background LLM reasoning:

1. **Sub-150ms Immediate Handshake:** The n8n Webhook node is configured with `Respond: Immediately (HTTP 200)`, acknowledging ManyChat within 150ms and satisfying the timeout condition.
2. **Background AI Synthesis (OpenAI):** In the background, n8n invokes OpenAI (GPT-4o) with custom system prompts to evaluate prospect intent and generate a contextual response.
3. **CRM Synchronization (Brevo):** Contact properties, lifecycle tags, and conversation notes are synchronized into Brevo CRM.
4. **Real-Time SDR Notifications (Slack):** If the prospect meets high-value enterprise criteria, an automated Slack alert with contact firmographics is pushed to sales reps.
5. **Asynchronous Callback (WhatsApp/ManyChat API):** Once generation is complete, n8n calls ManyChat's API asynchronously to deliver the personalized response.
